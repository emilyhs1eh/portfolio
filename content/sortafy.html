Sortafy

SORTAFY is an AI-powered photo management app created as my master’s capstone project. It leverages human-centered design principles to address the challenge of digital photo clutter. By combining my passion for photography with advanced AI technology, Sortafy streamlines photo organization, helping users efficiently manage their digital libraries while reducing storage costs.

Challenge

Last year 2023, over a trillion photos were taken worldwide. Each image tells a story — a birthday celebration, a sunset on vacation, or a simple moment of joy. Yet, how often do we revisit these memories? Most remain hidden, lost in a sea of digital clutter. In our fast-paced, image-saturated lives, users face overwhelming challenges:

  • Overwhelming digital clutter that leads to frustration.

  • Rising storage costs from unused photos.

  • Difficulty finding cherished memories amidst chaos.

Goals

  • Enhance Photo Sorting User Experience

  • Leverage AI Technology

  • Reduce Digital Clutter

(A) Research

Methodology

To uncover user pain points, I conducted through:

  • Desk Research: Analyzing existing solutions and market trends.

  • User Interviews: Engaging with 6 passionate photographers and photo enthusiasts to gain insights into their challenges and needs.

Key Insights

As I sifted through the feedback, several key pain points emerged:

  • Complexity
    Current solutions often overwhelm users rather than simplify their lives.

  • Storage Limitations
    Many users hit storage caps, leading to financial strain.

  • Cross-Platform Struggles
    Users desired a seamless experience that spanned their devices.

User Journey Mapping

(B) Ideation & Testing

How Might We…

With a clear understanding of user pain points, I asked myself: How might we reduce the effort required for users to organize their photos? This question became the foundation of my brainstorming sessions, leading to innovative ideas that harnessed the power of AI.

  • Insights 01

    • Users appreciate the swiping function to sort photos but suggest adding buttons to navigate to the next photo.
    • A confirmation warning before deletion and an "undo" option are needed to prevent accidental photo loss.
    • Enhancements like multi-photo selection and clearer navigation cues are recommended.

  • Insights 02

    • Users want explanations for AI suggestions to build trust.
    • Users like the AI tool but express a need for customization options, such as focusing on specific dates, to improve the sorting process based on personal preferences.

  • Insights 03

    • Privacy and Control Needs: There is a need for robust privacy settings and owner controls to manage access and edits, ensuring personal preferences are respected.
    • Introducing features like a voting system for edits could help resolve disagreements and streamline collaborative decision-making.

  • Insights 04

    • Some users desire the option to keep all variations within similar photo groups to ensure no photos are lost.
    • Users want the ability to manually adjust photo groupings and mark photos as “similar” for better personal oversight.

Key Takeaways from Usability Testing

After sketching initial ideas, I tested in two rounds with the same group of 6 participants. Gathering 3 crucial user feedbacks to refine the design goals further:

  • Desire for Larger Displays - Users wanted their photos to shine, preferring larger viewing areas for better visibility.

  • Simplified Choices - The decision-making process should be as simple as “keep” or “delete,” with an option to mark favorites later.

  • Clarity in AI Decisions - Users appreciated AI assistance but craved transparency —clear explanations of why certain photos were recommended for keeping or deleting.

Branding — SORTAFY: Where Memories Meet Innovation

Sortafy empowers passionate photographers to reclaim their memories through effortless photo organization. Our brand embodies the journey of transforming a vast collection of images into a manageable, organized experience. The circular symbol represents this process—like the moon, which grows fuller as it sifts through the clutter, illuminating the beauty of each captured moment.

At the heart of Sortafy are our core values: Snap, Sort, Satisfy. We celebrate the joy of capturing moments, provide an intuitive and efficient photo organization experience, and ensure user satisfaction by enhancing connections to memories, allowing users to effortlessly rediscover and cherish their favorite moments. Welcome to Sortafy, where memories meet innovation.

(C) Final Design

Feature #1 - SmartSort

This intelligent tool leverages AI to analyze photos, suggesting which ones to keep or delete by grouping similar images. It helps users navigate their collections with ease.

Key Highlights

  • Blurry and Similar Photo Filters

  • Timeline Sorting

  • AI Suggestions

  • Sorting Progress Indicator

  • Preview Before Deletion

Feature #2 - AlbumGenius

With Dynamic Album Creation, you can generate albums based on specific inputs like dates, categories, or themes. The enhanced Tagging feature utilizes advanced AI for precise photo retrieval, while the AI Album Review option allows you to review and edit AI-generated album results, ensuring your collections truly reflect your vision.

Key Highlights

  • Dynamic Album Creation

  • Enhanced Tagging

  • AI Album Review

By embracing its capabilities, Sortafy emerged not just as a tool for managing photos, but as a gateway for users to effortlessly reconnect with their cherished memories. In the end, this project has allowed me to fulfill a deeper purpose

— helping individuals fully embrace their love for photography and the stories each image tells.
