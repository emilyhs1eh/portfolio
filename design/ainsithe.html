Serving tea enthusiasts and investors in traditional puer tea and Taiwanese tea, my role was to design and launch a new e-commerce site that mirrors the immersive in-store experience.

Challenge

Although AINSI THÉ had a logo and a general understanding of its business mission, it struggled to clearly convey its core value of offering "drinkable puer tea" versus "investment-aged puer tea" in communications and tea ceremonies. To provide customers with a pleasant tea experience at home during the pandemic, we needed an online e-commerce platform for AINSI THÉ's customers.

Goals

  • Translate the in-store experience of puer tea online.
  • Enhance the conversion rate and brand awareness.
  • Reduce the high bounce rate and increase customer engagement.

(A) Understand

Our Customers - 50+ Interviews

We discovered that our customers need easier navigation, clear sections for key benefits of different puer tea sections and payment options, better display of product details, and higher-quality images for a stronger brand impression.

*Conducted user interviews during tea ceremonies as a tea sommelier

Our Customers Pain Points in Home Tea Brewing

Through testing, we understood that many users encounter key issues with buying products on our website: insufficient product information, inability to purchase small quantities of tea, and a slow checkout process that increases bounce rates.

Persona

Online shoppers, who are often less acquainted with in-store experiences, require more detailed instructions. In contrast, in-store customers benefit from immediate assistance and direct interaction with our products, including the opportunity to taste tea firsthand.

Our Target Audience: In-Store vs. Online Experiences

This user journey map was created to illustrate how our customers purchase tea products through our initial online website.

(B) Define

User Pain Points

  • Insufficient tea brewing information hinders home use.
  • High bounce rate due to a slow checkout process.
  • Inability to purchase small quantities deters flavor experimentation.

Design Goal

Improve the user experience by developing a clear information architecture and visual identity for product selections. This helps customers quickly find what they are looking for online, with redesigned checkout buttons, comprehensive product details, and tea brewing instructions that help recreate the in-store tea brewing experience.

Focus Area

  • Information Architecture
  • Shoppable Navigation
  • Member Account
  • After-Sales Service

(C) Ideate & Testing

Redesign the Information Architecture

By dividing the site into two sections, "Shop" and "Collection," users can easily navigate to their areas of interest, whether they are looking to purchase tea for drinking or for investment.

Challenges and Breakthroughs

While researching our ancient puer tea product, we discovered that 19.80% of our target audience is interested in investment puer tea. We organized our collection by decade, spanning from the 1920s to the 1990s, and provided detailed information on the value and history of each puer tea.

*Above sources from Google Analytics data of AINSI THÉ

Shoppable Navigation

The site features sticky "Add to Cart" and "Buy Now" buttons for viewing product information that stays at the top while scrolling, enhancing navigation with elegant motion.

Challenges and Breakthroughs

Based on our research into online shopping habits, I redesigned the shopping flow using wireframes that incorporate sticky buttons. The challenge was ensuring these buttons are visible without being intrusive during product page navigation, which was crucial for reducing checkout abandonment. User testing proved that this streamlined design speeds up purchasing for returning customers and improves information access for new users.

Adding Member Account

A member account system ensures that the registration process is user-friendly while clearly communicating benefits. Offering discounts, order tracking, and personalized recommendations can attract sign-ups.

Challenges and Breakthroughs

The process itself may be seen as complicated and can cause a higher bounce rate. It was essential to simplify registration and make the benefits immediately tangible, so we designed a one-click registration feature using social media or email verification.

*Member Account Create & Checkout Flow Prototype

After Sales Service

During COVID-19, maintaining customer engagement post-purchase was a challenge, especially when linking the in-store experience with the at-home experience. After-sales services such as exclusive content can bridge physical and digital interactions to sustain interest and loyalty.

Challenges and Breakthroughs

A significant breakthrough was the inclusion of a small card with a QR code inside each package, which customers can scan to access an exclusive brewing podcast. This approach enhances the educational value of each purchase and strengthens engagement by integrating interactive digital content with the physical product.

Design Solution

Insufficient Brewing Info → Better IA & Brewing Guide

To improve access to tea brewing information, we restructured the website's information architecture. The Shop section focuses on drinkable puer tea with detailed tasting notes and brewing instructions, while Collection focuses on aged investment puer tea with background information and expert advice on aging potential.

High Bounce Rate → Checkout Flow & Member Account

We optimized checkout buttons for faster transactions and added member accounts for discounts, order tracking, history, quicker re-purchases, and improved engagement.

Inability to Purchase Small Quantities → Custom Sample For Tasting

Customers can now select 10g samples of different teas, perfect for tasting or travel. This supports flavor experimentation and convenience while making the shopping experience more flexible.

Impact

Accomplishment in JUN 2021 - JUN 2022

Boost in Awareness and Conversions

Increased conversion rates by 30% within 60 days of launch and tripled e-commerce growth over a year.

Increased Customer Retention

By offering unique educational content through the brewing podcast, the brand can keep customers engaged beyond the initial purchase and foster a deeper customer relationship.

Boosted Brand Image

Offering a seamless integration of in-store quality and at-home convenience elevates the overall brand image, helping customers perceive the brand as innovative and customer-centric.
