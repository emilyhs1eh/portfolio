Challenge

In 2023, over a trillion photos were taken worldwide. Each image tells a story — a birthday celebration, a sunset on vacation, or a simple moment of joy. Yet, how often do we revisit these memories? Most remain hidden, lost in a sea of digital clutter. In our fast-paced, image-saturated lives, users face overwhelming challenges:

  • Overwhelming digital clutterthat leads to frustration.
  • Rising storage costs from unused photos.
  • Difficulty finding cherished memories amidst chaos.

Goals

  • Enhance the photo sorting user experience.
  • Leverage AI technology to reduce manual effort.
  • Reduce digital clutter while keeping users in control.

(A) Research

Methodology

To uncover user pain points, I conducted through:

  • Desk Research: Analyzing existing solutions and market trends.
  • User Interviews: Engaging with 6 passionate photographers and photo enthusiasts to gain insights into their challenges and needs.

Key Insights

As I sifted through the feedback, several key pain points emerged:

  • Complexity - Current solutions often overwhelm users rather than simplify their lives.
  • Storage Limitations - Many users hit storage caps, leading to financial strain.
  • Cross-Platform Struggles - Users desired a seamless experience that spanned their devices.

User Journey Mapping

Based on research insights, users who love taking photos expressed frustration with digital photo clutter, leading me to develop an intuitive AI-powered sorting feature. The user journey map illustrates how a user interacts with Sortafy to effortlessly organize their photos, showcasing moments of joy and frustration as they reclaim their cherished memories.

(B) Ideation & Testing

With a clear understanding of user pain points, I asked myself: How might we reduce the effort required for users to organize their photos? This question became the foundation of my brainstorming sessions, leading to innovative ideas that harnessed the power of AI.

Key Takeaways from Usability Testing

After sketching initial ideas, I tested in two rounds with the same group of 6 participants. Gathering 3 crucial user feedbacks to refine the design goals further:

  • Desire for Larger Displays - Users wanted their photos to shine, preferring larger viewing areas for better visibility.
  • Simplified Choices - The decision-making process should be as simple as “keep” or “delete,” with an option to mark favorites later.
  • Clarity in AI Decisions - Users appreciated AI assistance but craved transparency —clear explanations of why certain photos were recommended for keeping or deleting.

System Map

I mapped the relationship between photo input, AI grouping, review states, and album output to clarify how users move from unsorted clutter to a curated library.

Branding

SORTAFY: Where Memories Meet Innovation

Sortafy empowers passionate photographers to reclaim their memories through effortless photo organization. The circular symbol represents transforming a vast collection of images into a manageable, organized experience.

At the heart of Sortafy are the values Snap, Sort, and Satisfy: celebrating the joy of capturing moments, providing an intuitive organization experience, and helping users reconnect with their favorite memories.

(C) Final Design

Feature #1 - SmartSort

SmartSort leverages AI to analyze photos, suggest which ones to keep or delete, and group similar images so users can move through their collection with confidence.

  • Blurry and similar photo filters
  • Timeline sorting
  • AI suggestions
  • Sorting progress indicator
  • Preview before deletion

Feature #2 - AlbumGenius

AlbumGenius creates albums from inputs such as dates, categories, or themes. Enhanced tagging and AI album review help users retrieve, review, and edit generated collections.

  • Dynamic album creation
  • Enhanced tagging
  • AI album review

Next Steps

  • Gather more user feedback.
  • Expand tagging capabilities to enhance organization.
  • Integrate with social media platforms for seamless sharing.
  • Refine AI algorithms to optimize sorting.
  • Synthesize feedback to prioritize MVPs and build a cohesive design system.

Takeaways

Initially, my journey began with a simple goal: to find a more efficient way to organize photos, unaware of the transformative potential of AI technology. As I immersed myself in the experiences of passionate photographers, I discovered that AI could be the key to revolutionizing this process. By embracing its capabilities, Sortafy emerged not just as a tool for managing photos, but as a gateway for users to effortlessly reconnect with their cherished memories. In the end, this project has allowed me to fulfill a deeper purpose — helping individuals fully embrace their love for photography and the stories each image tells.
